\paragraph{DRL for UAV-assisted data collection.}
Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) is widely used for joint UAV trajectory and scheduling
problems, which are non-convex and combinatorial~\cite{luong2019applications,zhao2025survey,amodu2025survey}.
%%CHECK: ba survey đều ◐; chỉ dẫn chung, không trích kết luận cụ thể.
Bayerlein et~al.~\cite{bayerlein2021multi} train one double deep Q-network policy over a
range of scenario parameters (number, positions and data volumes of sensors, flight time,
start position) for single-shot data harvesting, with constant energy per step; they do
not compare against a policy trained on a fixed scenario.
%%SRC: docs/04 §5 hàng Bayerlein (PT (5), (13), §II-A, §VI-A, §VI-B). DAT là người đọc; DAT kiểm câu.
De~Souza Lamenza et~al.~\cite{lamenza2025} randomize sensor positions and the sensor count
in a visit-once task without an energy model.
%%SRC: docs/04 §5 hàng Lamenza (§1, §3.4, §4, PT (7)).

\paragraph{AoI-oriented UAV policies.}
Abd-Elmagid et~al.~\cite{abdelmagid2019deep} minimise weighted sum-AoI with a deep
Q-network for a fixed placement of at most three ground nodes, with a flight-time budget
instead of a propulsion model.
%%SRC: notes/reading/abdelmagid2019deep_FAO.md (§II-A, §II-B, §IV).
Yi et~al.~\cite{yi2020deep} use the rotary-wing power model only in a constraint and
fly at a fixed speed or hover; Sun et~al.~\cite{sun2025aoi} minimise the peak AoI
of laser-charged UAVs with a propulsion model but a constant speed, and list domain
randomization as future work.
%%SRC: docs/04 §5 hàng Yi 2020 (PT (1), (7), §II-A, Bảng I); notes/reading/sun2025aoi_FAO.md (§III-A, Bảng IV, §VII).
OH-DRL~\cite{ohdrl2025} reverses the roles of the two quantities, minimising flight energy
under a maximum average-AoI constraint.
%%CHECK: CHỈ tóm tắt (notes/reading/ohdrl2025_FAO.md). Không nêu gì thêm khi chưa có toàn văn.
The closest work is Yi et~al.~\cite{yi2023meta}: the UAV chooses its speed and heading,
propulsion energy depends on speed and acceleration, and a meta-learned policy is trained
over tasks with different sensor numbers and positions. Its generalization is measured as
the speed of adaptation with further training after a single task change, not as
performance without retraining under structured shifts.
%%SRC: notes/reading/yi2023meta_LDN.md (PT (9)–(10), (15a), §IV-A1, §IV-A2, §IV-B2, Fig. 8).
%%CHECK: Form B Yi 2023 còn là nháp chờ LDN duyệt; bản tạp chí 2025 chưa đọc.
Other meta-learning approaches adapt to relocated sensors~\cite{xiao2024meta}, to new
objective weights~\cite{sarathchandra2025age}, or to new weights and unseen rain
regions~\cite{eldeeb2026resilient}, again with further training on the new task.
%%SRC: docs/04 §5 hàng Xiao (Fig. 2–3), Sarathchandra (Fig. 4–6), Eldeeb (§V-B, §V-C, Fig. 5, 9).
%%CHECK: Xiao "cần đối chiếu PDF" (docs/04). Eldeeb: "vùng mưa chưa thấy" (docs/04) — LDN kiểm chữ "rain regions".

\paragraph{Generalization and evaluation practice.}
Domain randomization trains on a distribution of simulated environments so that a policy
transfers to unseen conditions~\cite{tobin2017domain}; in drone racing,
Liu~\cite{liu2024generalizable} finds that success decreases as test tracks move away from
the training distribution, without a fixed-environment baseline.
%%SRC: notes/reading/liu2024generalizable_FAO.md (§III-C3, §IV-A, §IV-B, Fig. 6, Fig. 10).
%%CHECK: tobin2017domain ✱ (VERIFY).
None of the works above reports results over multiple training seeds with confidence
intervals, which Agarwal et~al.~\cite{agarwal2021deep} recommend for DRL.
%%SRC: cột "Nhiều seed + CI" = Không ở mọi hàng docs/04 §5 đã đọc toàn văn (03/11). Kiểm lại khi có Gong, Lu, Toure.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
Table~\ref{tab:positioning} compares these works on six properties.
%%CHECK: thay câu dưới bằng câu định vị bản 3 (docs/01 §3) khi DAT viết. Hiện là bản 2 rút gọn.
Prior work has shown that one DRL policy can cover a range of scenario parameters for data
harvesting~\cite{bayerlein2021multi}, and that AoI- and energy-aware policies with speed as
an action can be adapted to new sensor layouts through meta-learning with further
training~\cite{yi2023meta}. What remains untested is whether a policy trained once with
domain randomization keeps its information freshness, without retraining, under structured
shifts in sensor layout, density, area and energy budget, compared with the same learner
trained on a fixed layout, over multiple training seeds.

\subsection{Limitations}
%% Nguồn: docs/01 §7, docs/02, docs/03 §3 và §10, notes/EXP-07_ablation_card.md.
Our conclusions are bounded by the following modelling and evaluation choices.

\emph{Simulation only.} All results come from a simulator; no flight experiment was
performed, and the causal statements we make hold only within the simulated model.

\emph{Channel.} We assume a line-of-sight free-space channel without blockage or
shadowing; a probabilistic LoS model~\cite{alhourani2014optimal} is a natural extension
that we did not implement.
%%CHECK: alhourani2014optimal ◐; chỉ trích như hướng mở rộng.

\emph{Geometry and mobility.} The UAV flies at a fixed altitude in a two-dimensional
area, chooses among eight directions and two discrete speeds ($V_{\mathrm{me}}$ and
$V_{\mathrm{mr}}$), and collects data only while hovering (fly--hover--communicate).
Wind, acceleration and turning costs are not modelled, and there is no constraint to
return to a charging point.

\emph{Single UAV.} Multi-UAV coordination, studied for instance
in~\cite{bayerlein2021multi,sun2025aoi}, is outside our scope.

\emph{Scope of the distribution shifts.} The four OOD suites are designed shifts in
spatial structure, sensor density, area and energy budget. Robustness to these shifts
does not imply robustness to other shifts, such as different channel conditions or
sensor traffic; in the larger-area suite the physical size of a grid cell also changes.

\emph{Baselines and tuning.} The heuristics read the true sensor positions and ages at
full resolution, which favours them over the learned policies. DR-PPO and Fixed-PPO share
the same default PPO hyper-parameters, without tuning; the comparison is therefore
balanced, but neither learner is shown to be at its best configuration.
%%SRC: docs/03 §2, §10; docs/PREREG.md §6 (PR #38, bài FJCAI không tinh chỉnh Optuna, DAT 03/11).
%%     Bản đầy đủ (có Optuna, ablation): khôi phục câu "equal budget ... ablation reuse" từ bản 22/10.

\emph{Statistical power.} Each learned method is trained with 10 seeds; with samples of
this size we rely on
permutation tests and bootstrap intervals and avoid strong claims when intervals overlap.
%%SRC: docs/PREREG.md §4–§5 (PR #38). Bản đầy đủ thêm số seed của ablation và quét λ.

\emph{Design parameters.} Several environment parameters (slot length, packet size,
grid resolution, reward weights) are design choices rather than values from the
literature; they are listed as such in Table~T1.
%%SRC: docs/02 (các giá trị "thiết kế"), docs/06 §3.
