\section{Related Work}
%% Owner: FAO. Built from the positioning matrix (docs/04 sec. 5).

\paragraph{DRL for UAV-assisted data collection.}
Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) has become a common tool for joint UAV trajectory and
scheduling problems, which are non-convex and combinatorial; recent surveys cover the
breadth of this line of work~\cite{luong2019applications,zhao2025survey,amodu2025survey}.
%%CHECK: ba survey đều ◐; câu chỉ dẫn chung, không trích kết luận cụ thể của survey.
Closest to our setting, Bayerlein et~al.~\cite{bayerlein2021multi} train double deep
Q-networks for single- and multi-UAV data harvesting on map-based observations centred
on the agent, and show that one policy can serve a range of scenario parameters,
including the number, positions and data volumes of sensors, the flight time and the
start position, with additional tests beyond the training range.
%%SRC: docs/04 §1 (✔ toàn văn, DAT đọc). DAT kiểm lại câu này với §IV–§V của [B21].
Their objective is the amount of data collected in a single mission, and the flight
energy per step is modelled as constant, a choice they motivate by the small difference
between hovering and horizontal flight.
%%SRC: docs/04 §1; docs/01 §3. %%CHECK: câu "khoảng 10%" trong docs/04 chưa có số mục; DAT ghi mục.
de Souza Lamenza et~al.~\cite{lamenza2025} also target a model that handles a flexible
number of sensors, in a visit-once data collection task.
%%CHECK: ◐, chưa mở được toàn văn (docs/04 §2). Bỏ câu này nếu tới W10 vẫn chưa đọc được.

\paragraph{Age of information in UAV networks.}
Age of information (AoI)~\cite{kaul2012realtime} measures the freshness of the most recent
update received from a source.
%%CHECK: kaul2012realtime ✱ (VERIFY trong references.bib).
Abd-Elmagid et~al.~\cite{abdelmagid2019deep} formulate the joint choice of UAV moves and
update scheduling as a finite-horizon MDP that minimises the weighted sum of AoI, and solve
it with a deep Q-network. The UAV has no propulsion-energy model, only a flight-time
budget, and each scenario uses a fixed placement of at most three ground nodes; results
are averaged over 1000 episodes without training seeds or confidence intervals.
%%SRC: notes/reading/abdelmagid2019deep_FAO.md (§II-A, §II-B, §III-B, §IV).
Yi et~al.~\cite{yi2020deep} combine trajectory and scheduling decisions to reduce
weighted AoI under an energy constraint.
%%CHECK: ◐ (ar5iv), docs/04 §1. Kiểm toàn văn: năng lượng là của UAV hay của cảm biến? bố trí cố định?
Sun et~al.~\cite{sun2025aoi} minimise the peak AoI of a multi-UAV network with laser
charging using MAPPO extended with temporal memory. Their energy model follows the
rotary-wing propulsion power, but the flight speed is held constant, so speed is not a
decision variable; sensor positions are fixed and robustness is examined only by
sweeping one physical parameter. The authors list domain randomization as future work.
%%SRC: notes/reading/sun2025aoi_FAO.md (§III-A, Bảng IV, §V-A, Fig. 4b, §VII).
OH-DRL~\cite{ohdrl2025} reverses the roles of the two quantities: it minimises UAV flight
energy subject to a maximum average-AoI constraint, with a hierarchical design in which DRL
selects the UAV's visiting policy and semidefinite relaxation handles scheduling and
STAR-RIS beamforming.
%%CHECK: CHỈ tóm tắt (notes/reading/ohdrl2025_FAO.md). Không nêu gì về tổng quát hoá của OH-DRL
%%       cho tới khi có toàn văn (câu hỏi A3 trong notes/2026-10-21_fao_can-DAT.md).
Sarathchandra et~al.~\cite{sarathchandra2025age} use meta-learning so that a DQN adapts
when the weighting between age and transmit power changes, i.e., the shift is in the
objective rather than in the deployment.
%%CHECK: ◐ (docs/04 §2, ma trận §5). Kiểm toàn văn trước khi giữ câu "objective rather than deployment".

\paragraph{Generalization of DRL policies.}
Domain randomization trains on a distribution of simulated environments so that the
learned policy transfers to conditions not seen during training~\cite{tobin2017domain}.
%%CHECK: tobin2017domain ✱ (VERIFY).
In drone racing, Liu~\cite{liu2024generalizable} regenerates randomized tracks and
obstacles for every rollout of PPO and evaluates one policy on increasingly shifted
tracks; its success rate decreases as the test tracks move away from the training
distribution, and no fixed-environment baseline or multi-seed analysis is reported.
%%SRC: notes/reading/liu2024generalizable_FAO.md (§III-C3, §IV-A, §IV-B, Fig. 6, Fig. 10).
%%     Bài ngoài phạm vi tổng quan hệ thống (docs/04 §6); chỉ dùng làm tham khảo phương pháp DR.
Because DRL results vary strongly across training runs, we follow the recommendations of
Agarwal et~al.~\cite{agarwal2021deep} and report per-seed bootstrap confidence intervals
and interquartile means.
%%CHECK: agarwal2021deep ◐. Câu này có thể chuyển sang Experimental Setup (LDN quyết).

\paragraph{Positioning.}

our study combines.
While parameter generalization has been shown for single-shot data
harvesting~\cite{bayerlein2021multi}, it remains unclear whether such policies stay robust
under structured distribution shifts when the objective is continual information freshness
and the propulsion energy depends on speed.

evaluates under structured shifts with multiple training seeds; the gap is also noted

\subsection{Limitations}
%% Nguồn: docs/01 §7, docs/02, docs/03 §3 và §10, notes/EXP-07_ablation_card.md.
Our conclusions are bounded by the following modelling and evaluation choices.

\emph{Simulation only.} All results come from a simulator; no flight experiment was
performed, and the causal statements we make hold only within the simulated model.

\emph{Channel.} We assume a line-of-sight free-space channel without blockage or
shadowing; a probabilistic LoS model~\cite{alhourani2014optimal} is a natural extension
that we did not implement.
%%CHECK: alhourani2014optimal ◐; chỉ trích như hướng mở rộng.

\emph{Geometry and mobility.} The UAV flies at a fixed altitude in a two-dimensional
area, chooses among eight directions and two discrete speeds ($V_{\mathrm{me}}$ and
$V_{\mathrm{mr}}$), and collects data only while hovering (fly--hover--communicate).
Wind, acceleration and turning costs are not modelled, and there is no constraint to
return to a charging point.

\emph{Single UAV.} Multi-UAV coordination, studied for instance
in~\cite{bayerlein2021multi,sun2025aoi}, is outside our scope.

\emph{Scope of the distribution shifts.} The four OOD suites are designed shifts in
spatial structure, sensor density, area and energy budget. Robustness to these shifts
does not imply robustness to other shifts, such as different channel conditions or
sensor traffic; in the larger-area suite the physical size of a grid cell also changes.

\emph{Baselines and tuning.} The heuristics read the true sensor positions and ages at
full resolution, which favours them over the learned policies. Hyper-parameters were tuned
with an equal budget for DR-PPO and Fixed-PPO on the in-distribution suite only; the
ablation variants reuse the DR-PPO hyper-parameters without separate tuning, so their
reported degradation is an upper bound on the harm of removing each component.
%%SRC: docs/03 §2, §3, §10; notes/EXP-07_ablation_card.md. Câu DQN: CHỜ quyết định A2 (tinh chỉnh DQN).

\emph{Statistical power.} Each learned method is trained with [n] seeds
([n] for ablations and the $\lambda$ sweep); with samples of this size we rely on
permutation tests and bootstrap intervals and avoid strong claims when intervals overlap.
%%Điền [n] từ docs/PREREG.md sau G1.

\emph{Design parameters.} Several environment parameters (slot length, packet size,
grid resolution, reward weights) are design choices rather than values from the
literature; they are listed as such in Table~T1.
%%SRC: docs/02 (các giá trị "thiết kế"), docs/06 §3.
